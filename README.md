## 🌌 Welcome to Iochem

**Iochem** is an advanced, ultra-high-fidelity interactive workspace designed to bridge the gap between microscopic atomic theory and visual macro-molecular simulations. Tailored for research-level exploration and intuitive playground interactions, Iochem turns complex chemical workflows, physics engine collisions, and reaction stoichiometry balancing into an elegant, tactile experience. 

---

## 🔮 Key Features

### 1. ⚛️ Relativistic Physics & Collision Sandbox Lab
*   **Tactile Atomic Physics Simulation:** Drop active elements, transition metals, organic compounds, and acid/base reagents directly into a frictionless physics sandbox.
*   **Kinetic Energy-Triggered Combinatorics:** Watch chemical reagents bounce, attract, and dynamically collide under Newton-Euler constraints to initiate real-time chemical synthesis.
*   **Adaptive Heat-Responsive Particles:** Reagents are textured with custom category colors (Water, Acids, Bases, Oxides, Salts, Organics) and scaled relative to their actual physical molecular mass.
*   **Procedural Smart Reaction Engine:** Go beyond static catalogs with procedural reaction predictions that automatically synthesized acids, bases, basic/acidic oxides, single-displacement hydrogen-evolved metals, combustion channels, and carbonate acidolysis.

### 2. ⚡ Autonomous Chemical Equation Balancer & Completer
*   **AI-Powered Predictive Synthesis:** Simply input any set of reactants, and the predictive engine auto-fills the thermodynamic products, balancing the multi-step stoichiometric coefficients instantaneously.
*   **Valency-Guided Molecular Integrity Checks:** Powered by carbon tetrad-bond systems, standard metallic indices, and electronegative matching, the platform audits molecular formulas on-the-fly and alerts you of non-stoichiometric valencies.
*   **Decomposition & Multi-Reactant Combustion Modules:** Balance anything from simple water split-ups to complex hydrocarbon-oxygen combustion equations (yielding perfectly structured carbon dioxide and water).

### 3. 🗺️ High-Contrast Interactive Periodic Table
*   **118-Element Spatial Grid:** Fully populated physical layout with custom category highlights for alkali metals, halogens, transition metals, and precious noble gases.
*   **Thermodynamic, Crystal, and Electronic Summaries:** Deep dive into atomic mass, electronegativity, electron configurations, and discover real-time suggested reaction partners directly linked to the Sandbox Engine.

### 4. 🌐 Real-Time Multi-Language Localization
*   **Unified Translation Architecture:** Seamless, instant switching between English (EN), Georgian (KA), and Russian (RU).
*   **Polished Desktop & Mobile Interface:** Intelligent viewport layout engine hides bulky brand titles and wraps translation features elegantly into settings dials when height exceeds width, giving maximum interactive real estate on mobile viewports.
